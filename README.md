# Customer Analysis

A beginner-friendly customer analysis project using Python and Pandas to explore customer spending behavior and purchasing patterns.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer data to identify high-value customers, spending patterns across gender and age groups, and customers with the highest purchase frequency.

## Business Problem

The business needs to understand its customers better in order to identify valuable customer segments and improve marketing and customer retention strategies.

## Solution

Customer data was analyzed using Python and Pandas.

The analysis includes:

* Customer spending analysis
* Purchase frequency analysis
* Spending by gender
* Spending by age group
* Identification of high-value customers
* Business insights and recommendations

## Key Results

* Highest-spending customer: **C009**
* Highest spending: **1,750**
* Highest-spending gender group: **Male**
* Highest-spending age group: **25–34**
* Customer with the most purchases: **C009**

## Business Insights

1. Customer C009 is the highest-spending customer and also has the highest purchase frequency in the analyzed dataset.
2. The Male customer group generated the highest total spending.
3. Customers aged 25–34 generated the highest total spending among the analyzed age groups.

## Recommendations

* Focus on retaining high-value and frequent customers.
* Consider targeted campaigns for the 25–34 age group.
* Segment customers based on spending and purchase frequency to create more personalized offers.
